I'm Aes Saputra, the person behind this garden. I use it to keep track of what I'm learning and the things I build.

Much of what I write here comes from working through technical questions. I keep notes on how software works, the choices behind it, and what helped when something went wrong. Writing gives me a reason to slow down and explain an idea in my own words.
## How I approach things

I prefer simple solutions that I can understand and maintain. Before adding a tool, I want to know what problem it solves and whether something I already have can do the job.

That also shapes this garden. I keep related notes connected so I can return to a question without starting over. The notes are useful to me first; I share them in case they help someone else working through the same thing.
