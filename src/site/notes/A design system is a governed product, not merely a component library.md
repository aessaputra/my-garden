A component library distributes interface code, while a design system coordinates broader product decisions.

It combines principles, tokens, patterns, components, content guidance, documentation, ownership, and contribution processes.

[USWDS describes](https://designsystem.digital.gov/about/what-is-the-design-system/) an active community around reusable solutions, rather than presenting code as the entire system.

Without governance, duplicated exceptions accumulate until shared components no longer represent reliable organizational standards.

[[Semantic tokens preserve intent across themes and platforms\|Semantic tokens preserve intent across themes and platforms]] connects shared design decisions directly to implementation.

[[Contribution criteria prevent design systems from becoming component dumps\|Contribution criteria prevent design systems from becoming component dumps]] controls evolution through evidence and review.

These layers create a maintained product that serves other product teams through stable contracts.

Judge maturity by adoption, ownership, feedback, and upgrade discipline, not simply by component count.
