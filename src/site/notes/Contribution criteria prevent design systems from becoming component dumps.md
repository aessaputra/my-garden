Shared repositories decay when every local solution enters the system without evidence, ownership, or maintenance commitments.

Contribution criteria require proposals to demonstrate recurring needs, consistency, versatility, usability, and implementation quality.

[GOV.UK reviews](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/community/contribution-criteria/) components and patterns before publication, including representative research with users who have disabilities.

This gate protects coherence, although excessive central control can delay useful experimentation and local learning.

[[A design system is a governed product, not merely a component library\|Shared product ownership with accountable maintainers]] sets coherent direction and sustainable maintenance.

[[Documented component states turn reuse into testable behavior\|Testable component documentation with runnable states]] supplies concrete review evidence for every proposed contribution.

Teams should incubate uncertain patterns locally, observe repeated use, then promote stable solutions with migration support.

A contribution process succeeds when it balances system integrity with a credible path for change.
